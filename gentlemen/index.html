Quick Facts

TTPs

  • Initial access via FortiGate CVEs or purchased/stolen credentials — rarely phishing
  • PowerShell, PsExec, WMI for discovery & lateral movement
  • Defender tampering, log & shadow-copy deletion for evasion
  • Go-based encryptor pushed domain-wide via modified GPO

IOCs

  • CVE-2024-55591, CVE-2025-32433, CVE-2025-33073
  • Unauthorized Cloudflare Zero Trust tunnels in victim environments
  • Wiped prefetch, RDP logs, PowerShell history
  • Deleted volume shadow copies

Targeted Sectors

  • Opportunistic, not sector-fixed — chosen by revenue and cyber-insurance coverage
  • Confirmed victims: energy (Complexul Energetic Oltenia)
  • Confirmed victims: technology / software consultancies (UK–Turkey chain breach)

Attack Patterns

  • Double extortion: exfiltration before encryption, leak-site posting backs the demand
  • Ransom sized to victim's likely insurance ceiling
  • Self-propagating encryptor can detonate within days of initial foothold
Section 01

Actor Profile

Origin & structure

  • Founded mid-2025 by hastalamuerte (also linked to alias zeta88) after a public split from the Qilin RaaS program
  • Ran as a closed operation through the summer before opening an affiliate program in September 2025
  • Recruited affiliates who previously worked with Embargo, LockBit, Medusa, and BlackLock

Targeting philosophy

  • Researches targets before committing resources — not a spray-and-pray operation
  • Favors organizations whose revenue and cyber-insurance coverage make a paid settlement likely
  • Initial access is almost entirely edge devices (unpatched FortiGate appliances) or purchased credentials — rarely phishing

Extortion model

  • Standard double extortion: data exfiltrated before encryption, leak-site posting backs the demand
  • Ransom demands reportedly calibrated to a victim's likely insurance ceiling using firmographic revenue data
  • Affiliates keep up to 90–93% of proceeds — an unusually generous split built to pull talent from rival programs

Section 03

Kill Chain & MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

The Gentlemen's kill chain leans almost entirely on unpatched edge devices and purchased credentials rather than phishing, then moves fast once inside affiliates are known to enumerate, evade, and detonate within days of gaining a foothold.

01

Initial Access

Exploit Public-Facing Application

FortiGate / Cisco appliances — most often CVE-2024-55591 (FortiOS/FortiProxy auth-bypass), alongside CVE-2025-32433 and CVE-2025-33073.

Valid Accounts

Purchased or brute-forced credentials — bought from infostealer-log markets, or brute-forced against exposed VPN/RDP when no edge device is exposed.

02

Execution & Persistence

Command and Scripting Interpreter

PowerShell & native CLI tools — disable security controls, enumerate Active Directory, identify privileged accounts before deployment.

Scheduled Task / Remote Access Software

Scheduled tasks & self-restarting services — often paired with a legitimate remote-access tool installed under a disguised name.

03

Discovery & Defense Evasion

Impair Defenses

Defender disabled, logs wiped — broad exclusions added, shadow copies deleted, prefetch/RDP logs/PowerShell history stripped to slow IR.

Exploitation for Privilege Escalation

UAC bypass & driver abuse — elevates a foothold to domain-level access before the encryptor is staged.

04

Collection & Exfiltration

Automated Exfiltration

Cloudflare Zero Trust tunnel — data staged and pulled out over an unauthorized tunnel registered inside the victim's own environment, before encryption begins.

05

Impact

Data Encrypted for Impact

Custom Go encryptor — hybrid Curve25519/XChaCha20 encryption, self-propagates via multiple lateral-movement techniques, typically pushed domain-wide through a modified GPO.

Section 05

Detection & Mitigation Priorities

Ordered by impact-to-effort ratio for a mid-sized enterprise.

  • P1Patch internet-facing FortiGate and Cisco appliances immediately, prioritizing CVE-2024-55591, CVE-2025-32433, and CVE-2025-33073.
  • P1Disable NTLMv1 and enforce MFA on every VPN and remote-access endpoint.
  • P1Alert on shadow-copy deletion, Defender tampering, and log/prefetch clearing as near-certain precursors to encryption.
  • P2Audit and remove unauthorized Cloudflare Zero Trust tunnels and unexpected remote-access tooling.
  • P2Move backup infrastructure behind dedicated, credential-rotated management VLANs isolated from the production domain.
  • P3Prepare an extortion response playbook in advance, including legal, comms, and law enforcement contacts.
